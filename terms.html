What TIGERDLE is

TIGERDLE is a free, independently-run fan project for Hull City AFC supporters. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Hull City AFC in any way. No official data feed, no club involvement, just a fan's own project.

Using the site

Everything on TIGERDLE is free to use. Accounts are for genuine personal use. Please don't create multiple accounts to game leaderboards, and keep league names and comments genuine and non-abusive (automatically checked, and manually reviewed where needed).

Accuracy

Player details, fixtures, and match results are entered by hand and best-effort, genuine mistakes can happen. The predictor and its scoring are for fun; nothing on this site should be relied on for anything beyond that, and there's no guarantee of accuracy or availability.

No money changes hands

TIGERDLE has no paid features, no betting, and no prize pots of any kind. Nothing here should be treated as gambling or a financial product.

Changes

This is an evolving personal project, features, scoring rules, and these terms may change as it develops. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
