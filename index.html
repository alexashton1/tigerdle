What is TIGERDLE?

TIGERDLE is a free, fan-made puzzle site for Hull City AFC supporters. Every day brings four separate games: a Wordle-style surname guesser, a squad-and-legends matching game, a clue-by-clue mystery player, and a goal reveal where you guess the opponent. None of it needs an account or costs anything.

Signed-in supporters can go further with the Predictor, picking Hull's starting XI, calling the scoreline, and predicting who scores. Points are scored automatically after every match, and there are private or public leagues to compete with friends. TIGERDLE isn't affiliated with Hull City AFC; it's built independently by a fan, for fans.

Frequently asked questions

What is TIGERDLE?

TIGERDLE is a free daily puzzle site for Hull City AFC fans. It includes four separate daily guessing games, plus a season-long match predictor and leagues to compete with friends.

How do you play the daily games?

Each day brings a new puzzle in TIGERDLE, the Hull City Wordle: guess a 5-letter Hull City surname in six tries, with tiles showing how close each guess was. Three more daily games sit alongside it: squad legends, a mystery player revealed through clues, and a matchday goal photo revealed by guessing the opponent.

When does a new puzzle come out?

A new TIGERDLE puzzle is released every day. All four daily games refresh together, so there's a fresh challenge in each one, every single day.

Are the answers only Hull City players?

Yes, every TIGERDLE answer is a genuine Hull City AFC player, past or present. The games draw from the club's full history, not just the current squad.

Is TIGERDLE free?

Yes, TIGERDLE is completely free with no ads and nothing to buy. The daily games need no account at all, and the predictor and leagues only need a free email sign-in.

Is TIGERDLE official or affiliated with Hull City AFC?

No, TIGERDLE is an independent, unofficial fan project built by a single Hull City supporter. It has no connection to Hull City AFC, its staff, or its official channels.
