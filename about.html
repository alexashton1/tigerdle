About & FAQ

What this is, who made it, and how it all works.

The short version

Tigerdle is a free, fan-made puzzle site for Hull City AFC supporters: four daily guessing games, team news after every match, a season-long predictor, and leagues to run with friends. It's built and looked after by a single fan, not a company, and it isn't affiliated with Hull City AFC in any way.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tigerdle affiliated with Hull City AFC?

No. This is an independent, unofficial fan project with no connection to the club, its staff, or its official channels.

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything's free, no ads, nothing to buy. The daily games need no account; the predictor and leagues need a free one.

Do I need to sign in to play?

Not for the four daily games, those work immediately, no account needed. Signing in (just an email link, no password unless you choose to set one) unlocks the predictor and leagues.

How does the predictor work?

Pick Hull's formation and starting XI, call the scoreline, and predict who scores. Points calculate automatically once the real result's in, and a handful of bonus wildcards are available a few times a season for anyone feeling bold.

Who built this?

One Hull City fan, as a personal project, not a business, not a company.

Something wrong, or an idea?

Get in touch: hello@tigerdle.com. Genuinely welcome.
