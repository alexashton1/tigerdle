Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Tigerdle is a free, fan-made project. This page explains, plainly, what information gets collected and why.

What's collected

If you never sign in: the four daily games store your streaks and stats only in your own browser, never sent anywhere. Basic, anonymous analytics (page visits, general location by country/region, device type) are collected via Google Analytics to understand how the site's used.

If you sign in: your email address, whatever display name you choose, your game stats, predictions, ratings, and league memberships are stored securely so they can follow you across devices. If you choose to set a password, it's stored securely and never visible to anyone, including the site's operator.

What it's used for

Purely to run the site: remembering who you are, calculating your predictor points, showing league standings, and sending the emails you'd expect (a sign-in link, an optional prediction reminder). Nothing here is sold, and nothing is shared with third parties beyond the services that make the site work (Supabase for the database and accounts, Google Analytics for basic usage stats, Resend for sending emails).

Cookies

A small amount of local storage and cookies keep you signed in and remember your daily game progress. Google Analytics sets its own standard analytics cookies. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Your data, your control

You can delete your display name, change your email preferences, or ask for your account and all associated data to be deleted entirely at any time, just get in touch and it'll be actioned promptly.
